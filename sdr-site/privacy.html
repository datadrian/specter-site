Privacy Policy

How SPECTER handles application, purchase, activation, support, and website data.

1. Local-first application data

SPECTER SDR processes spectrum samples, demodulated audio, decoded packets, receiver settings, sessions, evidence, reports, and Gallery content on your computer. This content is not uploaded to SPECTER during normal operation. You choose whether to export, transmit, or share it.

2. License activation

Activation sends the license key, product identifier, purchase email when entered, and a one-way machine identifier to the SPECTER license service. SPECTER stores the key record, product, purchase email, purchase reference, machine binding, status, and activation timestamps to deliver and enforce the license, prevent abuse, and support legitimate transfers.

3. Purchases

Stripe processes checkout. SPECTER receives the customer email, product, amount, currency, tax and payment status when applicable, Stripe checkout and payment references, and the accepted legal-document versions and acceptance timestamp. SPECTER does not receive or store full payment-card numbers.

4. Support

Support records can include your name, email, product, subject, messages, ticket history, machine identifier when submitted from the application, and diagnostics you choose to provide. Do not submit passwords, payment-card details, or private radio content. Transactional email is used to deliver keys, receipts, confirmations, and support replies.

5. Consent-based website analytics

Analytics run only after you select Accept Analytics. If accepted, the site stores a random first-party visitor identifier for up to one year, a session identifier for 30 minutes, a returning-visitor indicator, and campaign attribution in browser storage and first-party cookies. Events can include path, referrer, visit duration, download action, browser user agent, country inferred by the hosting provider, and campaign tags. Analytics are used to understand site and download activity, not for cross-site advertising. SPECTER does not sell or share this data for targeted advertising.

If your browser exposes Global Privacy Control or Do Not Track, analytics default to declined. You may withdraw consent below. Declining analytics does not affect checkout, downloads, support, or the application.

6. Hosting and security logs

Netlify and infrastructure providers may process IP address, request path, timestamp, user agent, approximate network location, and security logs to deliver and protect the site. These operational records are separate from optional site analytics and may be retained under provider policies and legal obligations.

7. Optional third-party services

Opening an aircraft map requests tiles from OpenStreetMap providers. Skywatch catalog refreshes request public orbital data from CelesTrak. Installing an optional Decoder Lab engine downloads a pinned package from the identified third-party project after you approve installation. Checking for updates contacts the official SPECTER release service. These requests expose ordinary network information such as IP address and user agent to the relevant provider. Radio content and Gallery evidence are not attached to those requests.

8. Local integrations

The optional SPECTER Imaging bridge uses the computer loopback interface and a temporary local token. It does not require a SPECTER cloud relay. Remote rtl_tcp, Icecast, rigctl, and SBS connections go to destinations configured by you.

9. Retention

Local application data remains until you delete it. License and transaction records are retained while a license is active and as reasonably needed for fulfillment, fraud prevention, accounting, tax, legal, and dispute purposes. Support records are retained as reasonably needed to provide service and preserve ticket history. Analytics identifiers expire as described above, while aggregate and event records may be retained for business reporting and security.

10. Your choices and requests

You can decline analytics, clear local browser storage, delete local recordings, operate offline after activation, and request access, correction, or deletion of personal information associated with your email, subject to identity verification and legal retention obligations. Depending on your location, you may have additional rights to restrict or object to processing or complain to a regulator.

11. Security and international processing

SPECTER uses reasonable administrative and technical safeguards, but no system is perfectly secure. Service providers may process information in the United States or other locations where they operate, subject to their contractual and legal safeguards.

12. Children and changes

The products and site are not directed to children under 13. Material policy changes will be posted with a new effective date. Changes apply prospectively unless law requires otherwise.

13. Contact

Privacy requests may be sent to support@specter-imaging.com or submitted through support.
