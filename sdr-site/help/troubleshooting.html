Troubleshooting

Diagnose receiver discovery, sidecar startup, USB stability, audio, recording, and activation problems.

Fewer receivers than expected

  1. Confirm every RTL-SDR Interface 0 appears in Receiver Setup with WinUSB marked ready.
  2. Connect the missing receiver by itself. If it works alone but not with the array, duplicate USB serials are likely.
  3. Give every dongle a unique serial using rtl_eeprom, one receiver connected at a time.
  4. Use a powered hub and split larger arrays across USB controllers when practical.

A receiver is listed but shows no signal

Confirm an antenna is connected and appropriate for the band, gain is not pinned too low, and the receiver is not temporarily controlled by periodic ADS-B or Search Director.

The waterfall is blank in Full Sweep

This is expected until the first pass completes. A full 24-1766 MHz sweep takes roughly 25 seconds on one receiver. The aggregate panel shortens the effective full-range pass by splitting it across the array.

Rendering feels slow

Check the aggregate status for WEBGL2 GPU or CANVAS FALLBACK. On fallback, reduce FFT zoom, increase the waterfall speed value, or hide the aggregate panel to return more workspace and render time to receiver cards.

No receivers found

  • Choose USB Hardware, not Simulation or None.
  • Verify WinUSB is bound to each RTL-SDR Interface 0.
  • Press RESTART SDR in the header.
  • Open the sidecar log from Settings or Receiver Setup and look for enumeration or driver errors.

Waiting for sidecar

Close other SDR software that may own the dongles, then use RESTART SDR. The radio sidecar shuts down with the app, self-terminates when the main process disappears, and stale sidecars are cleaned up at the next launch.

No audio

Open the Audio Mixer, start monitoring, verify the channel and master are not muted, raise squelch sensitivity if needed, and confirm the tuned mode and bandwidth match the signal.

Recording, Library, or report problem

Confirm free disk space and write access to Documents\SPECTER SDR\. Interrupted sessions remain visible in Library for recovery and review rather than disappearing.

Activation rejected

Confirm the key begins with SSDR-, internet access is available for initial activation, and the key is not already active on another computer. Use the support page for replacement-key help.
