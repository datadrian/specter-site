Decoder Lab and Research Labs

Decoder Lab handles known protocols and live packet output. Research Labs is optional and adds adaptive pattern discovery, distributed searching, and contextual checks.

A decoded packet, a recognized allocation, and an unresolved pattern are different results. SPECTER SDR labels them separately and does not present a frequency match as a validated decode.

Open Decoder Lab

Press DECODER LAB, select a protocol, receiver, and frequency, then review the antenna guidance. The workstation warns when the selected receiver's configured antenna role is a poor match.

Research Labs

Press RESEARCH and enable the master switch. Research controls persist instead of resetting when panels refresh.

Pattern Hunter

Learns a per-receiver baseline and flags departures such as chirps, Doppler-like drift, hopping, periodic bursts, wideband events, and otherwise unclassified novelty. The novelty threshold ranges from 30 to 99 percent.

Search Director

Distributes a plan across available non-ADS-B receivers, then restores their previous tuning when stopped.

Skywatch Correlator

Checks qualified findings against known allocations, tracked aircraft, cached CelesTrak orbital elements, multi-receiver confirmation, inferred bearing, and apparent movement. REFRESH TLE CATALOG updates the satellite catalogue.

A research score prioritizes evidence for review. It is not a probability and does not identify a cause.

Evidence and lawful use

Qualified findings are stored locally with their reasons, spectral signature, and contextual checks. SPECTER SDR is receive-only. Monitoring laws vary by country and band, and decoded pager traffic may contain private information. Confirm local law and do not redistribute private content.
