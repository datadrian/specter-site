Build the array map

Open ARRAY SETUP from the left drawer, press AUTO-FIT CONNECTED, then assign every receiver a persistent name, slot, antenna role, and bearing. Save the configuration when the physical layout is complete.

Antenna roles and bearings

  • Directional: points toward its configured bearing and contributes directional amplitude evidence.
  • Omnidirectional: provides a broad reference channel.
  • Vertical reference: provides a reference for vertically polarized signals.

Use matched antennas, feed lines, gains, and comparable placement when interpreting amplitude relationships.

Rosebud display

Press ROSEBUD to open the radial array in a separate window or route it to a second monitor. Multi-radio arrays show comparative amplitude, inferred bearing, movement, and confidence. A single-radio setup renders a full 360-degree waterfall rather than an empty array hub.

Focus and aggregate handoff

Double-click any receiver canvas to enter focus mode. In the aggregate full-speed spectrum, click a frequency to focus the receiver assigned to that slice.

Annotation

Press P, or use the toolbar, to enter pen mode. Draw freehand marks or circle regions with touch, stylus, or mouse. Undo and clear are available from the toolbar, and annotations are captured in recordings. PNG export preserves the current annotated view.

Improve bearing quality

  • Calibrate PPM against a known reference.
  • Keep gain settings and antenna systems consistent.
  • Confirm every bearing matches the physical placement.
  • Use array-linked tuning when comparing the same signal across receivers.
  • Treat bearing as comparative evidence, not a precision geolocation measurement.
