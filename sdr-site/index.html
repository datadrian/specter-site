// OPT-IN ADVANCED RESEARCH

Automate the search. Preserve the reasoning.

Research Labs stays out of the normal workflow until enabled. When active, it learns the local RF environment, distributes search bands across available radios, and checks qualified events against ordinary explanations.

PATTERN HUNTER

ADAPTIVE BASELINE

Learns each receiver's normal spectrum and flags explainable novelty, chirps, Doppler-like drift, hopping behavior, periodic bursts, and wideband events.

SEARCH DIRECTOR

MULTI-RADIO SURVEY

Assigns aerospace, digital discovery, or wide survey ranges across idle receivers, alternates sweep direction, and restores saved tuning when stopped.

SKYWATCH CORRELATOR

CONTEXT CHECKS

Compares findings with known protocol bands, live aircraft, visible satellites, array confirmation, bearing, and apparent movement before assigning a review status.

A research score is an evidence-prioritization value, not a probability and not a claim about an object's identity or cause.
