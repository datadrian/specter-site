01

Kinect V2 Field Rig - Full Build Guide

The classic paranormal investigation build. A portable, battery-powered Kinect rig you can carry into any location.

// WHAT YOU NEED
Microsoft Kinect V2
Xbox One sensor. Buy used on eBay or Amazon. Look for "Kinect for Xbox One" or "Kinect V2". Check it powers on.
~$60 to $100
Kinect USB Adapter
The Kinect V2 uses a proprietary connector - you need the official "Kinect Adapter for Windows" to connect it via USB 3.0. Essential, don't skip it.
~$15 to $30
Windows Tablet or Laptop
Must be USB 3.0. Recommended: Microsoft Surface Pro, ASUS ROG Flow Z13, or any modern Windows 10/11 laptop. The faster the better - SPECTER is processing-heavy.
Your existing device or $400 to $1,200+
USB 3.0 Powered Hub (optional)
If your tablet only has one USB-A port, a powered hub keeps power delivery stable to the Kinect. Unpowered hubs may cause drop-outs.
~$20 to $40
Camera Mount / Magic Arm
A small magic arm or articulating arm lets you position the Kinect independently of your tablet. 1/4"-20 thread. Attach to a tripod, light stand, or clamp it to a shelf.
~$15 to $35
Portable Battery Pack (optional)
For fully wireless field use, a 65W+ USB-C PD power bank (e.g. Anker 737 or similar) powers both your tablet and hub simultaneously. 4 to 8 hours field life.
~$60 to $100
// ASSEMBLY
1
Install Kinect for Windows Runtime - Download the free runtime from Microsoft (search "Kinect for Windows Runtime 2.0"). Install it before plugging in the sensor. This is a one-time system install.
2
Connect Kinect adapter - Plug the Kinect into the proprietary adapter, then connect the USB 3.0 end to your laptop or powered hub. Use a USB 3.0 port - USB 2.0 will not work.
3
Mount the sensor - Attach the Kinect to your magic arm or tripod via the 1/4" thread on the bottom of the sensor. Position it level at roughly chest height for best entity detection angles.
4
Verify in Device Manager - Open Windows Device Manager and confirm the Kinect appears under "Kinect Sensor Devices". If it shows an error, try a different USB 3.0 port.
5
Launch SPECTER - Open SPECTER and select Kinect V2 as your input. The depth feed should appear within a few seconds. You're live.
FIELD TIP Keep the Kinect's USB cable off the floor during investigations - cable movement can register as field disturbance events. Use velcro cable ties to secure it to your rig.

RealSense D435i - The Carry-Anywhere Build

The smallest, most capable SPECTER rig possible. Fits in a bag. Mounts to a laptop lid. No bulk, no battery pack, no proprietary hardware.

// WHAT YOU NEED
Intel RealSense D435i
Available new from Intel, Amazon, or Mouser. Compact stereo depth camera with built-in IMU. About the size of a USB flash drive housing. This is the whole sensor.
~$150 to $200 new
USB-C Cable (USB 3.1 Gen 1+)
The D435i connects via USB-C. Use the included cable or a quality USB 3.1 cable. Critical: a USB 2.0 cable will cause frame drop errors and instability. Check the cable spec.
Included / ~$10 replacement
Windows Laptop or Tablet
Any modern Windows 10/11 device with a USB-C or USB 3.0 port. Recommended: Surface Pro 11, Dell XPS 15, ASUS ROG Flow Z13. More CPU/GPU headroom = smoother SPECTER performance.
Your existing device
3M Dual-Lock or Command Strips (optional)
The D435i is small and light enough to mount directly to the lid of your laptop using 3M Dual-Lock strips. Face it forward and you have a hands-free investigation rig - just carry the laptop.
~$8 to $15
1/4"-20 Tripod Mount (optional)
The D435i has a standard 1/4"-20 tripod socket on the bottom. Mount it to any light stand, tripod, or magic arm for stationary deployment in a room.
~$10 to $20
// ASSEMBLY - LAPTOP LID MOUNT (RECOMMENDED)
1
Install Intel RealSense SDK 2.0 - Download from Intel's GitHub releases page. Run the installer. This installs the drivers and runtime SPECTER needs. One-time install.
2
Clean the laptop lid surface - Wipe the top edge of your laptop lid with isopropyl alcohol where you'll mount the sensor. Let it dry fully before applying adhesive.
3
Apply 3M Dual-Lock to the D435i base - Cut two small pieces, press firmly to the flat bottom of the sensor. Apply the matching strips to the laptop lid. Press together for 30 seconds. The sensor sits flush along the top edge of your screen, facing forward.
4
Route the USB-C cable - Run the cable along the lid hinge to your USB-C port. Use a small velcro tie at the hinge point so the cable doesn't stress the sensor connection when you open and close the lid.
5
Launch SPECTER - Open SPECTER and select RealSense D435i as your input. The depth feed and IMU telemetry will appear immediately. All six Anomaly Index channels are now live.
WHY THIS WORKS The D435i weighs 72 grams. Mounted to your laptop lid, your entire investigation system is one device you carry in one hand. Walk through a location, set it on a table, or hand it to a team member - the rig adapts to how you investigate.

HDMI Capture Input - Any Camera Build

Already running a full-spectrum rig or night vision camera? Feed it directly into SPECTER via a USB capture card and run the full detection pipeline on any footage.

// WHAT YOU NEED
USB HDMI Capture Card
Any USB capture card that appears as a webcam in Windows. Recommended: Elgato Cam Link 4K, Magewell USB Capture HDMI Gen 2, or budget options like Digitnow (~$25). 1080p/30fps minimum.
~$25 to $200
Your Camera
Any camera with HDMI output: Sony FX3/FX30, full-spectrum DSLR, dedicated night vision camera, or any broadcast/prosumer unit. If it has an HDMI out, it works.
Your existing gear
HDMI Cable
Standard HDMI to your capture card. Length depends on your setup - a 6ft cable is usually enough. Use a right-angle adapter if your camera's HDMI port is on a tight angle.
~$8 to $20
Windows Laptop or Tablet
Fast CPU recommended - HDMI input + SPECTER's full detection pipeline is processing-intensive. A dedicated GPU will noticeably improve performance at high resolutions.
Your existing device
// SETUP
1
Connect capture card to laptop - Plug the USB capture card into your laptop. Windows should recognise it automatically as a video capture device within a few seconds.
2
Connect camera via HDMI - Run an HDMI cable from your camera's output to the capture card input. Set the camera to output clean HDMI (disable on-screen menus if your camera supports "clean HDMI out").
3
Verify the feed in Windows Camera - Open the Windows Camera app and select your capture card as the source. Confirm the live feed appears. If it doesn't, check HDMI cable seating and camera output settings.
4
Launch SPECTER - Select HDMI Input and choose your capture card from the device list. SPECTER will begin processing the live feed through the full detection pipeline immediately.
PRO TIP Full-spectrum cameras (UV/IR converted DSLRs) pick up light sources invisible to the human eye. Combined with SPECTER's motion analysis and anomaly detection, you're running the most sensitive investigation setup available at any price point.
