RECORDING

Capture the field interface to MP4

RECORD A SESSION

  1. Click REC or press F9.
  2. SPECTER captures the app window to MP4 (H.264).
  3. Stop again with F9 or REC.
  4. If a fallback transcode runs, wait for the progress bar to finish before closing the app.
Bitrates are optimized for smaller files on long investigations, so an all-night session does not fill a drive.

RECORDING QUALITY TIERS

Configure your recording parameters within the Settings panel (accessible via Ctrl+Shift+G). SPECTER provides several quality tiers to fit your storage and analytical requirements:

Quality TierBitrateDescription
Standard8 MbpsOptimized for long overnight surveillance sessions to prevent disk exhaustion.
Broadcast16 MbpsIndustry standard high-fidelity output for detailed evidence and audio review.
Ultra28 MbpsMaximum image detail retention, perfect for frame-by-frame analysis of visual anomalies.

AUTOMATIC DETECTION CLIPS

When an active detector triggers, SPECTER automatically records an isolated 20-second high-resolution video clip of the event. Each clip is structured as follows:

  • 10 seconds pre-roll: Pre-buffer footage captured immediately leading up to the detection trigger.
  • 10 seconds post-roll: Post-buffer footage captured immediately following the trigger.

This automatic pre-roll and post-roll capturing ensures investigators never miss the vital physical context preceding or trailing a detection event.

TIMECODE & EVIDENCE METADATA

If you set a project start timecode and frame rate in Settings (Ctrl+Shift+G), recordings and captures share that same global timecode. In addition to being burned into the video visually, this global timecode is permanently stamped directly into each clip's evidence metadata. This allows investigators to effortlessly align SPECTER footage with external cameras, environmental logging hardware, and audio recorders during post-investigation review. See the field guide for details.

TRANSCODE PROGRESS

If the app has to fall back to a temporary WebM capture, it converts the file to MP4 after you stop. A transcode progress bar appears until the MP4 is ready. Do not close SPECTER until it finishes, or the final file may be incomplete.
