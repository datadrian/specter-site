RECORDING

Capture the field interface to MP4

RECORD A SESSION

  1. Click REC or press F9.
  2. SPECTER captures the app window to MP4 (H.264).
  3. Stop again with F9 or REC.
  4. If a fallback transcode runs, wait for the progress bar to finish before closing the app.
Bitrates are optimized for smaller files on long investigations, so an all-night session does not fill a drive.

TRANSCODE PROGRESS

If the app has to fall back to a temporary WebM capture, it converts the file to MP4 after you stop. A transcode progress bar appears until the MP4 is ready. Do not close SPECTER until it finishes, or the final file may be incomplete.

TROUBLESHOOTING

SymptomFix
Recording won't startCheck Windows screen-capture permission; run as a normal user (not an RDP-locked session).
Long wait after stopA transcode is in progress, watch the progress bar.
Huge fileUse a medium bitrate in settings; update to the build with the ffmpeg pipe.
No audioCheck the recording audio device in settings.
