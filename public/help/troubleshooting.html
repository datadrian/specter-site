DEPTH / 3D

SymptomLikely causeFix
"CONNECTING TO DEPTH SENSOR" overlaySensor booting or unpluggedWait 5-10 s; check USB 3.0; replug the sensor; verify the SDK/service for Kinect.
DEPTH view blackNo depth streamSelect the RealSense/Kinect source; check the performance preset isn't blocking depth.
3D view emptyIntrinsics not loadedRestart the app; confirm the sidecar/bridge is running (RealSense).
Flickering brightness/colors in live depthOld buildUpdate to the latest build (range smoothing fix). Keep RANGE near center.
Close objects white, far black (or reverse)RANGE slider off-centerReset RANGE to 50 (center).
Gallery DEPTH looks flat / wrongStatic PNG vs rawUse the DEPTH layer (re-rendered), not an old screenshot; move RANGE.
Gallery RANGE doesn't affect color depthOld buildUpdate, RANGE now affects both mono and false-color.
No 3D button in galleryOld detectionOnly detections captured after the 3D snapshot feature was added have one; capture new detections.
No D / 3D badge on a cardNo depth saved for that hitThe sensor wasn't streaming depth at detection time.
Remember: in DEPTH and DEPTH_3D, color always means distance. POINT_CLOUD is the mode that textures points from the live camera. See DEPTH & 3D controls.

PERFORMANCE & PRESETS

SymptomFix
Laggy 3D / low FPSSwitch the preset to Light or Balanced; disable ROOM_MAP.
No DEPTH_3D in the view cycleEnable the Show preset, or enable 3D views in advanced settings.
Detectors not responding at session startWait for the first camera frame; detectors run once the live feed is active.

RECORDING

SymptomFix
Recording won't startWindows screen-capture permission; run as a normal user (not RDP-locked).
Long wait after stopA transcode is in progress, watch the progress bar.
Huge fileUse a medium bitrate in settings; update to the build with the ffmpeg pipe.
No audioCheck the recording audio device in settings.

LICENSING & DEMO

SymptomFix
Demo expired immediatelyMachine clock is wrong; correct the system clock and reinstall the demo.
Key won't activateOne key per machine; email support with your SPTR- key.
Still shows DEMO after activatingRestart the app; verify the activation endpoint is reachable once for the online check.
Detectors seem quiet? Confirm the camera feed is active, and review your detector sensitivity in Settings. In licensed mode all detectors run at the thresholds you set in Settings.
