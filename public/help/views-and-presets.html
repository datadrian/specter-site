VIEW MODES & PERFORMANCE PRESETS

Cycle the views with V, and tune SPECTER to your hardware

VIEW MODES

Press V to cycle through the available views. The exact order depends on your sensor and performance preset.

ViewWhat you seeRequires
RGBLive color feed with overlaysRGB source
DEPTHFalse-color or mono depth mapDepth sensor
DEPTH_3DOrbitable 3D depth point cloudDepth sensor
POINT_CLOUD3D cloud colored from the live RGB feedDepth + RGB
ROOM_MAPAccumulating 3D room scanDepth + ROOM MAP mode
DEPTH_BLEND (optional)RGB with semi-transparent depth on topEnable depth blend view in Settings
The Show performance preset enables the 3D views and DEPTH_BLEND in the cycle.

For DEPTH and DEPTH_3D controls (RANGE, DEPTH COLOR, RESET, SAVE VIEW), see DEPTH & 3D depth controls.

PERFORMANCE PRESETS

Open Settings → Performance preset and choose the option that matches your machine.

PresetUse when
LightLaptop is struggling — depth-only path; RGB feed and color ML off.
BalancedDefault — RGB and depth balanced for most investigations.
ShowDemo reel or max quality — turns on the 3D views and DEPTH_BLEND in the view cycle.
Changing preset may require a feed restart. If views do not update, switch source (press S) or restart the app.
