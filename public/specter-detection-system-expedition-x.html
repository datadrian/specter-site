SPECTER on Expedition X

SPECTER was featured on Discovery's Expedition X, the paranormal investigation series led by host Josh Gates with investigators Heather Amaro and Phil Torres. On the program, Heather called the platform the SPECTER Detection System.

That name captures what the software is built to do. SPECTER is not a single meter or camera effect. It is a coordinated investigation system that puts live imaging, environmental telemetry, audio review, remote monitoring, and evidence capture into one field interface.

If you searched for the SPECTER system seen on Expedition X, this is its official product site. The publicly available Windows application is named SPECTER Imaging.

What the SPECTER Detection System does

SPECTER Imaging is designed for investigators who need more than an isolated handheld reading. It coordinates multiple evidence channels while preserving the original visual, audio, and sensor context around an event.

  • Depth and SLS-style figure analysis: live depth-camera views with neural skeletal tracking and 3D reconstruction.
  • Environmental correlation: wireless SPECTER Nodes contribute proximity and magnetic-field telemetry to the investigation timeline.
  • Thermal and camera monitoring: combine supported thermal sources, RGB cameras, and an IP Camera Wall with up to eight feeds.
  • EVP Audio Lab: record, review, isolate, and export audio evidence with waveform and spectrogram views.
  • Automatic evidence capture: detections can preserve the relevant frame, telemetry, classification, and timestamp for later review.
  • Remote field displays: route the Evidence Wall and Instrument Bay to additional screens and monitor supported channels from a phone.

The result is a traceable investigation record rather than a collection of unrelated device readings. Investigators can review what happened, when it happened, and which channels changed at the same moment.
