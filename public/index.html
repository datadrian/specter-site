// SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

Every meter. Every channel. Every anomaly.

◈

ANOMALY INDEX

Composite confidence score — weighted blend of candidate region scoring, inverse field coherence, and baseline deviation. Threshold-triggered DETECTION EVENTS at 40%, 60%, and 80% confidence.

⬡

SLS NEURAL TRACKING

Neural network keypoint detection maps humanoid figures in real time. Kinematic Coherence metric penalizes false detections — figures that don't move like real people don't register as anomalies.

▦

RGB-D DEPTH FUSION

Every captured frame is paired with a complete spatial depth map. Archive entries with a depth badge carry full 3D geometry — not just a flat image. Reviewable in post.

≋

PARTICULATE FLOW VECTOR

Dense optical flow across every pixel block. Reports dominant direction in degrees and magnitude in pixels/frame. A sudden 90° rotation indicates something moved through the field that wasn't the smoke.

◎

FIELD COHERENCE

Variance of the optical flow field. A clean smoke cloud has coherence near 1.0. When something passes through, coherence drops sharply. Below 0.5 for more than half a second — the field is disturbed.

⟁

DETECTION ARCHIVE

Every detection event is logged autonomously with snapshot, detection type, intensity class, and confidence percentage. Nothing is lost in the field — full recall on every investigation.
