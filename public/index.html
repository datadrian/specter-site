// SYSTEM CAPABILITIES

When something manifests — you'll know.

SPECTER doesn't wait for you to notice something. It watches everything simultaneously, logs every anomaly, and surfaces evidence you'd have missed.

◈

ANOMALY INDEX

A composite confidence score built from six live environmental metrics. When the index crosses 40%, 60%, or 80%, SPECTER logs a CLASS-I, CLASS-II, or CLASS-III detection event — automatically, with a full evidence capture.

⬡

SLS NEURAL ENTITY TRACKING

Neural network keypoint mapping identifies humanoid apparitions and entities in the depth field in real time. Kinematic Coherence scoring filters static objects — only anomalies that exhibit movement patterns consistent with an entity presence are flagged.

▦

RGB-D SPATIAL CAPTURE

Every detection event is preserved with a full spatial depth map embedded alongside the RGB image. Unlike flat stickman screenshots, your evidence carries real 3D geometry — reviewable, measurable, defensible.

≋

FIELD DISTURBANCE VECTOR

Dense optical flow analysis across the full frame. When something moves through the field — visible or not — it disrupts the flow signature. SPECTER reports direction and magnitude, timestamped and logged. Investigate after the fact.

◎

FIELD COHERENCE MONITOR

Residual hauntings and intelligent manifestations disturb the ambient optical field differently. Field coherence drops sharply when an entity passes through. Sustained coherence collapse below 0.5σ is automatically flagged as a residual disturbance event.

⟁

AUTONOMOUS EVIDENCE ARCHIVE

Every detection event is catalogued without investigator intervention — snapshot, detection class, entity confidence score, field metrics at time of event. Nothing escapes the archive. Review your full investigation after, evidence intact.
